## Actual responsibility shape

The organization is deliberately smaller than the role catalog looks:

- the 10 outcome identities are durable controller bindings for acceptance and
  regression continuity; they never execute work merely because a historical
  configuration record contains a model label;
- Mika, Shared Windows Executor, and Quality Checker are the three maintained
  candidate cores, with Mika limited to intent, serious ambiguity, exceptions,
  and verified explanation;
- Architecture, Company Audit, Data Sync, Product/UI, Release/Runtime, and
  Security/Privacy remain conditional packets invoked only for unique named
  evidence or judgment;
- Phone Farm Application and Phone Fleet Reliability remain external Mac-only
  boundaries represented in the catalog; this Windows proof did not inspect or
  operate Phone Farm;
- Mac alert analysis remains a skill-only packet and creates no seat.

Repository knowledge, provider procedures, UI craft, reconciliation steps,
release checklists, and evidence formats remain selectively loaded skills.
Standing responsibility, maintained configuration, and invocation frequency
remain separate concepts.

## Collaboration and squad behavior

The controller owns every move. Each route has one accountable outcome, no
more than one primary executor per host, ordered participant stages, at most
two simultaneous workers, one distinct final Quality Checker, no Mika relay,
and one automatic consolidated correction. A contributor is added only when
it produces unique named evidence. A graph-required function may be satisfied
by an exact skill packet when independence is not required; that shared packet
never counts as independent review.

All 19 locked cases have exact participant order, squad, accountable identity,
host, optional shared-skill packets, and release-stage declaration. The three
routine routes are T09, T11, and T13; the remaining locked cases use the
cross-boundary template. Cross-domain cases stay controller-unbound until a
first-divergence receipt identifies one outcome. The credential/privacy case
remains a read-only controller preflight rather than acquiring execution
authority.

Product/UI uses the minimum route:

| Situation | Route |
|---|---|
| Exact functional acceptance and current design-system behavior | Shared executor with Product/UI skill, then independent QC |
| Interaction or information behavior requires judgment | Product/UI specifies, shared executor implements, independent QC decides |
| Brand, preference, or business tradeoff cannot be derived | Mika presents one bounded owner decision; execution remains blocked |

The independent company-audit route is Company Auditor followed by Quality
Checker. It stays read-only and distinct from issue-level acceptance review.

independent reviewer fail closed.
