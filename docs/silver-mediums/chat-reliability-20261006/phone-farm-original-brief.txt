- Typography

- Container logic
- Reading order
- Interaction clarity
- Consistency

- Use of negative space
- Visual cohesion
- Attention to detail

KAYA’S SPECIFIC WORKFLOW

Kaya manages Joy and James and prepares a weekly Trial Reels report.

Each week, she needs to determine:

- How many Trial Reels were published on each account
- How many Trial Reels on each account exceeded 10,000 views
- The view counts of those viral Trial Reels

She currently checks the phones account by account. Connection-route problems, lag, inconsistent mouse scrolling, and lost connections can cause her to lose her place and begin counting again.

The intended future workflow should establish, for each selected week and account:

- Trial Reels expected
- Trial Reels sent for publishing
- Trial Reels confirmed on the platform
- Trial Reels still unconfirmed
- Exact published reels
- Stable platform post identity or permalink
- Current view count
- Trial Reels exceeding 10,000 views
- Time of the latest platform observation
- Weekly report completeness

Normal software must perform this reconciliation.

Kaya should manually inspect only genuine exceptions that the system cannot establish, such as:

- A completed publishing job with no matching platform post
- Incomplete provider information
- Multiple possible post matches
- A post that disappeared after it was observed
- A temporarily unavailable platform or provider

Every manual resolution must produce a visible saved result and evidence trail.

Kaya should never need to remember where she stopped counting.

This should become a dedicated Trial Reels assurance and weekly-report workflow.
